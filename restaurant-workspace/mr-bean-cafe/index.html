The Shastri Nagar Ambiance

Warm Velvet Booths & Sizzling Skillet Aromas

Nestled right off the Dussehra Ground belt in Shastri Nagar, Mr Bean Cafe & Restaurant is designed as an intimate culinary sanctuary away from Jammu's busy thoroughfares.

From candlelit table corners for anniversary dates to festive booths for lively birthday celebrations, our guests cherish the gentle acoustic jazz soundtrack, warm caramel lighting, and the irresistible aroma of golden waffles and sizzling iron platters.
