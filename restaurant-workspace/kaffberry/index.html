THE CRAFT OF TIME

48-Hour Cold Fermentation & French Normandy Butter

At Kaffberry, true culinary luxury cannot be rushed. Our sourdough starters are nourished with mineral spring water and stone-ground organic flours, then rested for 48 hours at controlled cold cellar temperatures to develop light digestibility and intricate lactic aromas.

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our master bakers hand-laminate French puff pastries with 84% Normandy Beurre d'Isigny, ensuring shatteringly crisp outer crusts with delicate, honeycomb air pockets within.

🌾 Wild Natural Sourdough Starter & Himalayan Spring Water
🧈 84% Normandy Beurre d'Isigny Butter Lamination
🔥 Stone Hearth 450°C Wood-Fired Blistering
