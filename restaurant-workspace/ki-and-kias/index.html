OUR HERITAGE & INTENTION

Where Mindful Dining Meets Dogra Hospitality

Founded on Canal Road with a simple conviction: the greatest meals are unhurried, honest, and shared in comforting surroundings.

In a world hurried by screens and haste, Ki & Kia's was imagined as a calm harbor. Framed by leafy views of the canal walkway, our kitchen bridges contemporary European culinary craft with Jammu's legendary warmth.

We celebrate clean provenance: our saffron travels straight from Kishtwar's high valleys, our herbs are harvested fresh every sunrise, and our chefs hand-roll pasta ribbons from durum wheat flour daily.
