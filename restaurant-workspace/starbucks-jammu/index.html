THE THIRD PLACE PHILOSOPHY

Where Global Heritage Meets Himalayan Warmth

In the heart of Jammu's premier retail destination, Starbucks offers a calm haven to think, create, converse, and belong.

Located prominently on the Ground Floor of Wave Mall, Narwal/Channi Himmat, our store is crafted as an architectural tribute to the spirit of Jammu. Matte dark obsidian surfaces, warm walnut woods, and deep Siren forest greens create an atmosphere of quiet luxury.

We honor every nuance of the coffee craft: 100% fair-trade Arabica beans roasted with decades of mastery, milk steamed to silky microfoam sweetness, and dairy alternatives including certified oat, almond, and soy.
