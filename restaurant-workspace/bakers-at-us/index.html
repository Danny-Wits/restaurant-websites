The Philosophy

An Ode to Sourdough, Stone & Time

In a world hurried by chemical improvers and industrial shortcuts, we chose the reverent path of the French boulanger.

Every loaf begins 36 hours before it reaches our stone hearth in Channi Himmat. Flour from non-GMO heritage wheat fields, pure mountain water, mineral salt, and our living wild levain starter. We fold, shape, and cold-proof each batch with patience.

For our patisserie, we marry classic Parisian techniques with local terroir: Kashmiri saffron from Pampore, wild walnuts from Kishtwar, and hand-selected vanilla pods from Madagascar. Here in Sector 1, we offer Jammu a sanctuary of taste, quiet luxury, and honest gastronomy.
