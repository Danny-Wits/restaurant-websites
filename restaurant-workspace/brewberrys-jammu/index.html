The Apsara Road Legacy

Jammu's Sunlit Urban Youth Sanctuary & Workspace

Situated right in the bustling heart of Gandhi Nagar, Brewberrys is built for genuine conversations, laptop work sessions, and lively post-college catch-ups.

We ditched dim and sterile spaces for sunlit buttermilk warmth, energetic tangerine accents, and an acoustic Indie soundscape. Whether you need a peaceful morning pour-over corner or a 6-person booth for birthday monster shakes, you're always welcome here.
