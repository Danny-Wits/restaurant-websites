The Apsara Road Legacy

Jammu's Urban Youth Sanctuary & Workspace

Situated right in the beating pulse of Gandhi Nagar, Brewberrys is built for genuine conversations, laptop work marathons, and after-college catch-ups.

We ditched sterile aesthetics for matte dark charcoal surfaces, warm barista glow, and an acoustic soundscape of indie tracks. Whether you need a solitary pour-over desk or a 6-person booth for birthday monster shakes, you're always home here.
