The Rooftop Sanctuary

Where Historic Jammu Meets Celestial Dining

High above the commercial energy of Bahu Plaza, the air cools, the skyline widens, and time decelerates.

Open Cafe & Dining was designed to honor Jammu’s geography. To our east lies the legendary Bahu Fort, bathed in amber radiance each night; below us whispers the historic River Tawi; and on clear twilight hours, the silhouette of the Trikuta mountains watches over the horizon.

Our culinary philosophy centers on fire, smoke, and freshness. Communal tapas for the center of the table, blistering Neapolitan pies right from the hearth, delicate Pan-Asian dim sums, and tender slow-braised Kashmiri shank — all accompanied by crafted zero-proof mixology.
