The Rooftop Sanctuary

Where Historic Jammu Meets Celestial Dining

High above the commercial pulse of Bahu Plaza, the air cools, the skyline expands, and dining becomes a celebration.

Open Cafe & Dining was designed to honor Jammu's unique geography. To our east stands the legendary Bahu Fort, bathed in amber radiance each twilight; below us whispers the historic River Tawi; and on clear evenings, the silhouette of the Trikuta mountains crowns the horizon.

Our culinary philosophy centers on fire, smoke, and vibrant freshness: communal tapas for sharing, blistering Neapolitan pies right from the hearth, delicate Pan-Asian dim sums, and tender slow-braised Kashmiri mutton shank — all accompanied by crafted zero-proof mixology.
